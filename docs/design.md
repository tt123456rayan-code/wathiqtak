# وثيقتك - Design Direction

## Visual Reference

The interface is inspired by the national youth identity used by Himma: high-contrast black, Jordanian green, red accents, white surfaces, and restrained gold highlights. The project keeps the Wathiqtak name, logo, and product identity independent.

## Palette

- Ink black: `#111111`
- National green: `#007a3d`
- Alert red: `#ce1126`
- Warm gold: `#b58a2a`
- Paper: `#ffffff`
- Soft background: `#f6f7f8`
- Warm surface: `#f7f2ea`

## Interaction

- Buttons use clear hover lift and quick press feedback.
- Page sections enter with a short fade-up animation.
- Hero elements animate in sequence on first render.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## UI Rules

- RTL remains the default.
- Cards stay at 8px radius to match the current system.
- Android/PaddleOCR future capabilities remain presented as future plans, not active features.
- The product remains local-first and privacy-forward.
